📖 Manual — How This Tracker Works

A quick walkthrough of every section, so nothing feels like a mystery button.

👑 HUD (top of page)

  • Rank / XP bar Your overall level, driven by checklist completion, tests logged, and streak.
  • Streak Consecutive days you've marked yourself present. Missing a day resets it.
  • Mark Present Tap once per day to log attendance and keep your streak alive.
  • Battle Tag A name you set so you can appear on the shared Leaderboard.

📊 Subject Completion Cards

  • The cards under the HUD show each subject's checklist % and chapters completed.

🎬 Stories Tab

A themed recap card for each subject (and Advanced), plus your all-time question count for it, pulled from the Daily Qs log. The same count appears as a small badge on each subject's nav button.

⚖️ Subject Balance Nudge

Appears automatically if one subject is far ahead of another (25%+ gap in checklist completion). It's a nudge to stop over-preparing one subject while neglecting another — a common cause of lopsided scores.

💾 Auto-Save & ⬇ Backup / ⬆ Restore / 🖨 Weekly Report

  • Auto-Save Every checklist tick, test entry, and daily log is saved automatically to this device's local storage the instant you make it — nothing to remember, no sync step, and it works fully offline.
  • Download Backup Optional — exports everything (checklists, tests, streak, daily questions) as a .json file, useful if you're moving to a new phone or want an extra copy.
  • Restore Backup Loads a previously downloaded .json backup, replacing current data on this device.
  • Weekly Report Opens a printable summary of the last 7 days — checklist progress, tests logged, streak, and present days. Use your browser's Print → Save as PDF to share it with a parent or mentor.

📝 Daily Qs Tab

Log your day-to-day practice: pick a subject, difficulty level, how many questions you attempted, and which exam it was for (JEE Main, JEE Advanced, or a custom "Other" exam). Every entry feeds the subject totals shown on the Story panel and tab badges, and counts toward your daily streak.

⚛ 🧪 ➗ Physics / Chemistry / Maths Tabs

Each chapter has a checklist (Reference Book, NCERT Theory, PYQs by year range, Notes, Lecture). Ticking items updates that chapter's %, the subject summary, and your overall XP.

🌌 JEE Advanced Tab

A separate, tougher checklist layer for chapters you're pushing to Advanced level, with its own counters and its own Weak Spot Radar (Anomaly Scan) — kept independent from the Mains-only Danger Zone.

🎯 Battle Log Tab

  • Danger Zone The most critical Mains chapters right now — badly scoring or fully checklist-complete but never tested. Only these entries get a "try a 50-question test" suggestion.
  • Chapter Skirmish Log Log per-chapter test results to track topic-level performance.
  • Final Zone Log Log full-length mock test results.
  • Mock Score Trend A line chart of your mock % over time — log at least 2 dated mocks to see it.

🏆 Booyah Badges Tab

Achievements you unlock through consistent progress, plus the Weak Spot Radar — chapters dragging your average down across both checklist and test performance.

🌐 Leaderboard Tab

Compares Battle Tags across everyone using this tracker — overall by average mock score, and subject-wise by checklist completion.
